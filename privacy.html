Privacy Policy

What stays on your device, and the one request the currency tool makes.

Last updated: October 2026

No account

UnitPulse does not ask you to register, and it does not keep a profile of you on a server.

What this browser stores

The last units you chose, the decimal setting, cached exchange rates, and your time-zone city list are stored in this browser only (localStorage). Amounts you type are not written there. Clearing site data for this site removes them.

Currency rates

The currency converter requests rates from api.frankfurter.app. That address redirects to api.frankfurter.dev without the headers a browser needs to follow the redirect, so the page then requests the second address directly. Either request reveals your IP address to Frankfurter and its host. The response is a table of reference rates. Other converters do not contact a server.

Advertising

Pages include slots for Google AdSense. The publisher ID on this site is the placeholder ca-pub-1234567890 until a real ID is added, and the ad script is not loaded. If ads are turned on later, Google may use cookies as described in its own policy. You can review ad personalisation at Google ad settings.
