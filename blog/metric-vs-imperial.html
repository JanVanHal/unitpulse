Metric vs imperial: a quick guide for travellers

TravelAugust 18, 2026·2 min read

By UnitPulse team

One country measures the road in miles and the next in kilometres. A recipe says cups, a shop sells grams, and the oven speaks Fahrenheit. You do not need the whole history of the two systems. You need a few anchors that are exact.

Length

The international inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres. A foot is 12 inches, so it is exactly 30.48 centimetres. A mile is 1,760 yards, which comes out to exactly 1.609344 kilometres. A person who is 6 ft 2 in is 187.96 cm.

The nautical mile is a different unit, used for sea and air distance. It is exactly 1,852 metres, a bit longer than a statute mile. If a chart says miles and you are not sure which kind, check before you convert.

Road signs in the United States and the United Kingdom are in miles. Most of the rest of the world, including Thailand and continental Europe, uses kilometres. Speed limits follow the same split.

Weight

The international pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. An ounce is 1/16 of that pound, about 28.35 grams. In Britain, body weight is still often given in stone. One stone is 14 pounds, about 6.35 kilograms.

A metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms. It is not the US short ton (2,000 pounds, about 907 kilograms). If a vehicle or a quote says “ton”, ask which one.

Volume, the easy place to get caught

A US pint and a UK pint are not the same drink. A US pint is about 473 millilitres. A UK pint is about 568 millilitres. The gallons differ by the same ratio: a US gallon is about 3.785 litres, and a UK gallon is exactly 4.54609 litres. Fuel economy inherits the split, which is why miles per gallon has a US figure and a UK figure.

In the kitchen, UnitPulse treats a US cup as 240 millilitres (the nutrition-label cup) and a metric cup as 250 millilitres. A US teaspoon in the tool is about 4.93 millilitres, and a US tablespoon is three of those. Many metric recipes instead use a 5 ml teaspoon and a 15 ml tablespoon. The gap is small for a pinch and real for a large batch.

Temperature

Water freezes at 0 °C and 32 °F, and boils at 100 °C and 212 °F. Human body temperature is about 37 °C, which is 98.6 °F. A moderate oven at 180 °C is 356 °F. For a rough mental step from Celsius to Fahrenheit, double the Celsius figure and add 30. That puts 20 °C near 70 °F. The exact conversion is 68 °F. Fine for deciding whether you need a coat, not for a science class.

What to actually do on a trip

Convert the one number in front of you, and notice which gallon, pint or ton the source means. The length, weight, volume and temperature tools use these anchors, and each one remembers the units you last picked.
