FAQ

Which inch and which mile are these?

The international inch is exactly 2.54 cm, so a foot is exactly 30.48 cm. The international mile is exactly 1.609344 km.

What is a nautical mile?

A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 metres. It is the unit used for sea and air distance, and it is a little longer than a statute mile.

How do feet and inches work?

There are 12 inches in a foot. You can type 6 feet and 2 inches, or type centimetres and read them back as feet and inches. Extra inches, such as 14, are fine. The reverse conversion carries them into feet.

Can I type a decimal comma?

Yes. 1,5 and 1.5 are both one and a half. If you type both marks, the last one is the decimal mark.
