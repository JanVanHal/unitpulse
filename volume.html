FAQ

Why are there two cups?

The US nutrition-label cup is 240 ml. A metric cup is 250 ml. Pick the one your recipe uses. The cup option in the lists follows that choice.

How do US and UK pints and gallons differ?

A US fluid ounce is about 29.57 ml and a UK fluid ounce is about 28.41 ml. A US pint is 16 US fluid ounces (about 473 ml). A UK pint is 20 UK fluid ounces (about 568 ml). A US gallon is about 3.785 litres. A UK gallon is 4.54609 litres.

Is a teaspoon 5 ml?

The teaspoon and tablespoon here are US customary: about 4.93 ml and 14.79 ml, and three teaspoons make a tablespoon. Many metric recipes use 5 ml and 15 ml spoons instead.

How are the ingredient grams calculated?

The From amount is turned into millilitres, then multiplied by a typical density. A US cup of plain flour is taken as 120 g, white sugar 200 g, butter 227 g and uncooked white rice 185 g. Milk is 1.03 grams per millilitre. These are kitchen figures. How you pack the cup changes flour in particular.

Which tablespoon is this?

The US tablespoon, three US teaspoons. It is not the 15 ml metric tablespoon and not the UK tablespoon used in some older recipes.
