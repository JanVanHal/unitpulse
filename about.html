About UnitPulse

UnitPulse is a free set of conversion tools for money, length, weight, temperature, cooking measures, land area, speed, fuel economy and time zones.

Our mission

Make everyday conversions fast and obvious, with the factors stated in the open. The arithmetic runs in your browser. Currency rates, when you are online, come from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter. Nothing here needs an account.

UnitPulse is the sister site of NetPulse, which covers network tools. The two sites share one design so they read as one suite.
