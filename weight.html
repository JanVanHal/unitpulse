FAQ

Which pound is this?

The international avoirdupois pound, exactly 0.45359237 kg. An ounce is one sixteenth of that pound.

What is a stone?

A stone is 14 pounds, about 6.35 kg. It is still used for body weight in Britain.

Is a tonne a US ton?

No. This tonne is the metric tonne, 1,000 kg. A US short ton is 2,000 pounds, about 907 kg.

How do pounds and ounces combine?

The extra fields add pounds and ounces into one weight, and they turn kilograms back into pounds and ounces. 16 ounces become another pound.
