FAQ

How big is a rai?

One rai is exactly 1,600 square metres. It is 4 ngan, or 400 square wa.

What is a square wa?

A linear wa is 2 metres, so a square wa is 4 square metres. In a land quote, “wa” almost always means square wa.

How do I read “2 rai 1 ngan 50 wa”?

Add the parts: 2 × 1,600 plus 1 × 400 plus 50 × 4 = 3,800 square metres. Use the three boxes under the main converter for that sum.

How do rai compare with acres and hectares?

A hectare is 10,000 square metres, which is 6.25 rai. An international acre is about 4,047 square metres, or about 2.53 rai. These figures explain a listing. They are not a boundary survey.
