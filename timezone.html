FAQ

Does this handle daylight saving?

Yes. The conversion uses your browser’s time-zone data through Intl.DateTimeFormat, so London, New York and Brussels move when those regions change the clocks. Bangkok and Tokyo do not use daylight saving.

Is the clock time in my laptop’s time zone?

No. The date and time you type are read in the city marked as the source, even if you are sitting somewhere else. “Use as source” on another row re-reads that same clock time in the new city.

Are my cities saved?

Yes, in this browser only, along with which city is the source. The date you type is not stored. Clearing site data removes the list and the page returns to the six default cities.

What happens in the missing hour when clocks spring forward?

Once a year some cities skip an hour, and in autumn one hour happens twice. If you type a clock time that does not exist, the page says so and the other cities follow the moment your browser returns. The overlapping hour follows one of the two possible offsets.
