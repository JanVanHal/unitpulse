FAQ

What formulas does this use?

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Kelvin is Celsius plus 273.15. The reverse formulas are the same relationships rearranged.

Why is 100 °F shown as 37.78 °C?

100 °F is 37.777… °C. At two decimal places that rounds to 37.78. The line under the result shows the value before that rounding.

What is absolute zero?

0 K, which is −273.15 °C. The converter will not go below that.

What do the reference buttons do?

Freezing is 0 °C (32 °F). Body temperature is 37 °C (98.6 °F). A moderate oven is 180 °C (356 °F). The button fills those Celsius values in for you.
