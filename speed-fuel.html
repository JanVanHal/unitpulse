FAQ

How big is a knot?

One knot is one nautical mile per hour, exactly 1.852 km/h. It is the usual unit for boats and aircraft.

Why are there two miles-per-gallon figures?

A US gallon and a UK gallon are different sizes. The US gallon is about 3.785 litres. The UK gallon is 4.54609 litres. The same car therefore has a higher mpg number in the UK unit.

Why is 10 L/100 km about 23.52 mpg (US)?

Miles per US gallon equals about 235.215 divided by the L/100 km figure. 235.215 / 10 is 23.52 at two decimal places. The line under the result shows more digits.

Which direction means more efficient?

A lower L/100 km number is more efficient. A higher mpg number, and a higher km/L number, are more efficient. The units run in opposite directions, which is why the same trip can look “better” or “worse” after a conversion.

What is km/L?

Kilometres per litre. It is 100 divided by the L/100 km figure, so 10 L/100 km is exactly 10 km/L.
